# Zils public seed: soup-c1 language known family (30 October 2026)

Three public demonstration cases for the known exam family `language-known-choice-s1`
(category language, Choice). Each case shows an English verb lemma and asks which of four
word forms carries a named inflection (simple past, present participle, third-person singular
present, past participle or base form). The four options are native forms of the same lemma.

`seed/` holds the exact `zils.base_exam seed` output (`cases.jsonl`, `registry.json`,
`manifest.json`). Gold labels are copied from the UniMorph English lexicon; no label was chosen
by a language model. These three cases are demonstrations only; exam questions stay private.
