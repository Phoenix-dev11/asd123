# Crisscrossed Captions — recorded grant and source limits

Attribution: Google Research, Crisscrossed Captions (CxC) dataset. Primary repository: https://github.com/google-research-datasets/Crisscrossed-Captions . Primary grant location recorded in the recipe: https://github.com/google-research-datasets/Crisscrossed-Captions/blob/master/LICENSE . Converted source used here: https://huggingface.co/datasets/DolphyWind/cxc-sts-sentences .

The existing recipe and earlier seed card record the primary CxC grant as permitting use for any purpose. The complete grant text and its exact copyright/attribution wording were not cached in the supplied distribution inputs; this package does not invent or reconstruct them. A mirror is not independent proof of upstream rights.

Three native caption pairs are retained. Their native human mean similarity judgments are quantized to six levels, 0..5, by the frozen Decimal round-half-up rule; no caption imagery is included. Caption wording is not newly paraphrased for publication.
