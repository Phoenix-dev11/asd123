# Original computed public fixtures

The arithmetic, coverage-count and three tools families use original project-generated public fixtures and computed gold. No original ASDiv text, QNLI pair or Glaive answer is copied for these public fixtures. The coverage fixtures use the exact same private_gold.coverage recipe identified by the recorded generator hash; the public seed does not expose private scenario reservations or tool catalogs.

This distribution wrapper does not assign a new blanket license to project-authored fixture text or task-format material. The owner must choose/document its publication terms as part of final disposition. Source-specific third-party terms remain separate and are not overwritten by any eventual package-level terms.
